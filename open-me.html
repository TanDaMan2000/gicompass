Why it matters

Colorectal cancer screening, in four facts.

Start at 45

Most adults at average risk should begin colorectal cancer screening at age 45.

Often no symptoms

Colorectal cancer may not cause symptoms at first, which is why screening matters.

Find it early

Screening helps find cancer earlier, when treatment is more effective.

Prevent it entirely

Screening can also prevent cancer outright by finding precancerous polyps.

Always talk to a doctor for medical advice and screening guidance.
